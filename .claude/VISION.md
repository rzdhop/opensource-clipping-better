# VISION — rzdhop AI

An open-source content factory run by one person (Rida) from a phone, on cheap infrastructure.

- **Clips** turns long videos (podcasts, talks, streams) into vertical short clips with karaoke subtitles,
  face-tracked framing, B-roll, BGM and ready-to-paste metadata. It runs on a CPU-only VPS and on Colab/Kaggle,
  analyses with free hosted LLM tiers, and never depends on downloading from a platform that may block it
  (local-first: the video and transcript can come from Rida's own tools).
- **AI stories** produce serialized ~60-second vertical episodes (fruit-people telenovelas first, any universe)
  with consistent characters and locked voices. The value is consistency across a season and retention structure,
  not "one prompt → one video". Claude is the writer and director in a claude.ai chat; the showrunner connector
  only makes, checks and cuts on rented GPUs billed by the second. Rida decides at six gates and reviews results,
  not prompts.

Constraints that shape every choice: money is spent only where it shows on screen (a few dollars an episode); every
clip is real motion with real voices, never stills or narration over images; the owner is not watching the
machine, so failures must be loud and explained in plain words; nothing is guessed silently.
