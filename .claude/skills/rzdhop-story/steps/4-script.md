# Story, step 4: the script (free)

You write the episode in full and Rida corrects it: the 90-second beat template, cliffhanger first, the lines in
the story's language, every line sized to the clip that will say it, and any new place. Free.

## When to use

- Episode 1, once the cast is locked (sheets, pictures, voices).
- Episode N+1, after `steps/7-next-episode.md` and Rida's pick of a direction.

## Read first

- `store_read 00-brief.md` (the chosen concept, the last 5 s of episode 1, the language), `04-season.md`,
  `memory.md` (from episode 2).
- Every `02-cast/<char>/sheet.md` (Wants, Fears, Secret, How they speak) and the `03-places/` that exist.

## Propose

No questions first: the full script, ready to read, with its choices visible (the length, the places, the register
of the lines, the cliffhanger). Then: "Here is episode N — correct any line or shot."

## Template

**The season** (`04-season.md`, episode 1 only, then kept up to date): `## Arc` in two sentences; `## Episodes`,
10+ one-liners — the hook block (E1–3), the reversal, the all-is-lost, the payoff — and the cliffhanger shape of
each, rotating: revelation / reversal / deadline / intrusion.

**The beats of one episode** (≈ 75–90 s, 10–13 clips, 16–24 lines — the characters talk a lot, Rida 2026-10-09):

| Time | Beat | Clips | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Hook: mid-conflict, one sharpening line | 1 | 5 s |
| 5–15 s | Setup: what is at stake | 1–2 | 10 s, three lines |
| 15–55 s | Escalation: 3 beats, each raises the stakes | 4–5 | 10 s, two or three characters, three lines |
| 55–60 s | The turn: a choice | 1 | 5 s, one character |
| 60–80 s | Peak: the shareable confrontation, in a busy place, people watching | 2 | 10 s |
| 80–90 s | Cliffhanger: cut before the reaction + "Part 2" | 1 | 5 s |

How the episode feels (every story, every universe):

- **Two or three characters per clip** in about 7 clips of 10; a 10 s clip carries three short lines (up to 22
  words in all). A clip with one character is for a big moment (a confession, a threat, the cliffhanger).
- **How each line is said** goes next to it (furious, whispering, voice breaking, smug…), and **each shot has one
  physical action** (a hand slammed on the table, tears, papers thrown, a door slammed). Emotions are big but
  believable: a telenovela, never a gag.
- **Busy places**: most scenes happen where people are (an open space, a café, a party, a street, a hall); the
  peak happens in front of witnesses. An empty place is a choice for an intimate scene, not the default.
- **Text on screen** is welcome when it tells the story at a glance: a sign, a folder, a phone screen, in one or
  two words.
- No silent shot unless the scene needs one: someone talks in nearly every clip. The assembly adds the faster cuts
  by punching in on each speaker; you do not write extra shots for that.

**The script** (`epNN/script.md`), one block per shot, in order:

```
# <Title> — episode <N>

## s01 — <place id> (5 s)
*(action — <what the bodies do: Marie-Jeanne slams her hand on the table; the room freezes>)*
- **<Name>** *(how it is said)*: <the line, in the story's language>

## s02 — <place id> (10 s)
*(action — …)*
- **<Name>** *(how it is said)*: <line>
- **<Name>** *(how it is said)*: <line, a second speaker in the same clip>
- **<Name>** *(how it is said)*: <line>
```

**A place** (`03-places/<place id>/plate.md`), for every place the script uses that has no plate yet:

```
# <Place name>

## Setting
<two sentences that fill the frame: 6–10 concrete things at three depths (foreground, middle, deep background),
colours, the light source and the time of day; a short sign may be spelled out in capitals ("a pink neon sign
reading CAFÉ"). No people here.>

## Extras
<3–6 background people from the universe's `## Extras`, each with head and clothes, and the one silent thing they do
while scenes play here (sit at their desks, walk past with coffee, glance over); "none" only for a place meant to
feel empty>
```

## Checklist

- [ ] The cliffhanger (last shot) is written first and matches the season map's shape for this episode.
- [ ] The hook (s01) is mid-conflict and readable with the sound off; no establishing shot, no title card.
- [ ] 16–24 lines, 3–12 words each; about 7 shots in 10 have two or three characters.
- [ ] Budget per shot: a 5 s clip says ≤ 10 words in all, a 10 s clip ≤ 22 (≈ 2.4 words/s after a beat of
      silence). Count the words of every shot's lines together.
- [ ] Every line says how it is said; every shot has one physical action; a silent shot says what the face shows.
- [ ] Every speaker has a locked sheet and a locked voice; every place has a plate with a full `## Setting` (no
      people in it) and its `## Extras`; most scenes are in busy places.
- [ ] Each character sounds like their `## How they speak`; nobody explains the plot.
- [ ] From episode 2: the recap is in the first line, not a recap shot; the open threads of `memory.md` move.

## Gate question

"Here is episode N — correct any line or shot. Once you say OK, I make the whole episode and come back with it."

## What to show Rida

The script as it will be heard: shot by shot, the lines in the story's language, the silent shots in one line, the
cliffhanger last, and each shot's length. The season map once, for episode 1. Before you go on, one line: what the
episode will cost (keyframes + clips + voices, ≈ $1–3) — then go on without waiting.
