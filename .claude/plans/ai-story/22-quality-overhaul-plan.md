# Plan 22 — AI Story quality overhaul: faithful concepts, structured scripts, premium writing, native speech on Veo 3.1, lower spend

Date 2026-10-04. Approved by the human the same day (the chat); the working copy of the plan.

## Context

The human watched the agent-mode episodes and the reference video (TikTok @muffindrama, 52 s, 3D lipstick characters, one continuous confrontation, ~15 shots of 2–5 s, native speech with lips in sync, burned subtitles, no narrator) and reported four defects:

1. **The texts are poor and unstructured** and "the content line does not explain the story". Root cause: `_E2_ASK_TEMPLATE` asks for "1 to 4 short spoken lines … at most 22 words; reference lines run 3-8 words", the narrated template adds "2 to 4 short lines in the whole episode … each a punch", E1's scene `summary` is "at most 15 words" with no cause or effect, each E2 call sees only the previous scene's last line, nothing asks for complete sentences or a reason behind a demand, and J1 cannot flag a line that adds nothing. The writers are free-tier models (`DEFAULT_STORY_LLM_CHAIN`: nemotron-3, mistral-medium, gemini-3.5-flash-lite).
2. **The lipsync is trash.** Ambience mode tells Veo "no voices, nobody speaks" (`prompting.AUDIO_CLOSING`), the clip is in fact silent seedance on this host, the mouth movement is invented, Kling LipSync moves lips to a flat TTS read after the fact and fails on fruit heads. Face and voice were never one performance.
3. **Concept proposals drift from the user's idea.** `build_c1` says "Invent exactly 1 original concept" at temperature 0.9; the seed is one data line ("Seed idea from the user: …", cut at 120 words) between the style line and a 24-title avoid list; `schemas.c1_errors` checks structure only.
4. **Paying more for less.** The pipeline buys six paid steps (sheets, plates, props, a keyframe per shot + redraws, clips, lipsync) around a cheap silent clip; creators buy one clip at subscription prices and pick takes by eye.

Facts checked today that the plan rests on:
- `GEMINI_PAID_API_KEY` is **not set** in `data/settings.json` (FAL_KEY, GOOGLE_API_KEY, OPENROUTER_API_KEY are). The quality preset's `first_with_audio` policy has always fallen back to seedance; Veo has never run here (A-127). No STT key is set either; the `.env` Groq key answers 401.
- A paid Gemini text model cannot run on the `gemini` provider: `free_tier=True` makes `is_free_link` true and RC-V4 says free Gemini chains never read the paid key. A sibling provider is needed.
- `GeminiVeoAdapter._body` hard-codes 720p; Lite's body must stay byte-identical (stored clip hashes).
- Render timing is TTS-driven (`timeline.build_timeline` → `timing.episode_pass`; `steps/render.py:216` refuses a line without audio).
- Veo 3.1 on the Gemini API: standard `veo-3.1-generate-preview` $0.40/s, fast `veo-3.1-fast-generate-preview` $0.10/s (720p), lite `veo-3.1-lite-generate-preview` $0.05/s; audio always on; `durationSeconds` 4/6/8 (8 forced with 1080p or reference images); ≤ 3 reference images (standard, fast); "English fully supported, other languages not evaluated"; "Use quotes for specific speech".
- Text: Gemini 3.8 Flash $0.75/$3.75 per M until 2026-12-31, then $1.50/$7.50; 3.1 Pro $2/$12.
- Caps are global Settings (`budget.Budget`, 13 call sites); a profile's `cap_usd` is read only by the agent estimate and the keyframe fix.

The human's answers (2026-10-04): Veo 3.1 **Fast** by default, standard as a per-story switch; **French**, probe first; **Gemini 3.8 Flash** for the important calls; caps **10 / 20 / 60**; spend less.

Further answers (same day): **"the content line" = the lines the characters say** — "not well written, not humanly understandable"; stage 3's E2v3 line rule is therefore the primary fix and the walk's first look. The human **adds both keys** (paid Gemini, Groq or Mistral for STT) in Settings. The human **wants a bring-your-own-clips mode**: the app produces very crafted, detailed prompts and the image shots; they generate the clips (and, when they choose, the images) on their own subscriptions — **Google Flow (Veo 3.1)** and **Higgsfield / Freepik** — and upload them; the app builds the rest. The paid Veo API links stay as an **optional automated route, after the manual mode**. Assessment: this fits the architecture — every generation step already goes through a link of a chain, so "the human" becomes one more link (`manual/upload`) and the writing, storyboard, prompts, native take, subtitles, render and publish stay the app's; it also removes the clip bill (≈ 85 % of an episode's cost) and lets the human pick takes by eye, which is what the reference creators do.

Decisions taken on the design's open points (recorded in ASSUMPTIONS at implementation): caps stay global Settings (the human sets 10/20/60); standard speech (≈ $17/episode) is refused by the per-episode cap with the number and stays probe-only until the human raises the cap for a story; the confrontation format is the wizard's suggestion when the native-speech profile is picked, always a choice (DEC-268); off-screen speech over reaction shots is a later probe; writing v3 stamps new stories and is a per-story opt-in on the generation profile card for existing ones (DEC-241's regenerate path); the 2027 price doubling is accepted and revisited then; Veo's person policy may refuse young characters in schools, the probe tells; the probe runs the $2.80 variant.

### Stage 2 — concept fidelity
**Goal.** With a seed, every card tells the user's brief; the ten cards differ in angle, never in premise; each card is rule-checked then judged; the card shows whether it kept the brief; agent mode's `params.count` kept.
**Files.** `clipping/aistory/context.py`, `prompts.py` (`build_c1_v2`, `build_c1j`, budget rows), `steps/concepts.py`, `schemas.py` (optional card `brief_fit`), `steps/bible.py` (B1v3 sees the brief), `steps/story_fast_track.py` (stops on a drifted card), `web/dashboard/src/pages/story/steps/ConceptsStep.jsx`.
**Design.**
- Gate: non-empty `seed_text` and `generation_profile.writing == "v3"` (new optional key; `store` stamps new stories). Without it C1 is byte-identical (new RC-W2).
- `_BRIEF_WORD_LIMIT = 400` for C1v2/B1v3 (`_SEED_WORD_LIMIT` 120 stays for old prompts); `INPUT_BUDGET["C1v2"] = 2000`, `MAX_TOKENS["C1v2"] = 700`; with a brief the avoid list keeps only this story's own cards.
- C1v2 ask (the brief first, fenced `<<< >>>`): "Write exactly 1 concept … that tells this brief (call n of N). The brief is binding. Keep exactly what it gives: every named character (same name, role and relationships), the setting, the premise and its central conflict, the genre, the tone, and every event it describes. Invent only what it leaves open. Never rename, replace or drop a named character, never move the story elsewhere, never change what the conflict is about. This call's angle: {angle} — the angle chooses which side of the brief the concept leads with, never the premise. … logline: one complete sentence, at most 30 words: who wants what, who stands in the way, and what is at stake … cast_sketch: every character the brief names comes first, with the brief's name and role". `C1_ANGLES` (10): played straight / open on its first confrontation / from the antagonist's want / the secret under the premise / a ticking clock / a public humiliation at stake / an unexpected ally / who holds power flips / a mystery of origins / betrayal from someone close; `count: 1` takes angle 1; temperature 0.7.
- Rule check inside `call_json`'s validator (DEC-259's told-why retry): `context.brief_entities` (capitalised tokens not sentence-initial, quoted spans, words after « appelé·e / named / called », stop-list) must appear folded in title/logline/world/cast; error "$.cast_sketch: the brief names X; the concept never does -- keep it".
- Judge C1J (one premium call per card, `MAX_TOKENS` 200): `kept` + `missing[]` (≤ 5 × 12 words). Not kept → the C1 call is asked once more with the misses as refusal reasons; still not kept → the card is stored with `brief_fit = {kept: false, missing, checked_by, checked_at}`, never silently; agent mode stops: "The concept drifted from your brief: …".
- B1v3: the brief block under the concept, "Keep the brief's names, setting and conflict".
- Dashboard: "✓ Kept to your brief" / "⚠ Drifted: …" on the card.
**Risk.** Proper-noun false positives in French (sentence-initial skip + stop-list, tested). ≈ $0.02 of judges per batch.
**Verification.** New `tests/test_story_concepts_brief.py` (prompt pins, angle n, rule refusal, judge retry then flag, `count: 1`, no-seed byte-identical); `test_story_prompts.py::test_c1v2_budget_with_a_400_word_brief`; payload contract. At risk: `test_story_steps.py`, `test_story_fast_track_story.py`.
**Rollback.** Remove the `writing` stamp; `brief_fit` is optional (RC-M3). **RC:** RC-A4, RC-E1, new RC-W2.

### Stage 3 — script structure v3 and the confrontation format
**Goal.** A dramatic spine per episode; cause-and-effect scene summaries; every line a complete sentence that advances; E2 sees the whole dialogue so far; the word budget matches the format; a ≈ 50 s confrontation format; J1 judges all of it; the dashboard shows the episode's logline.
**Decision.** New prompt ids `E1v3/E2v3/E3v3/J1v3` selected by `writing == "v3"` for any format, plus a new template `confrontation_50s_v2` (per-story choice, DEC-268). v1/v2 prompts, `timing.word_budget`, `shots.py` and the goldens stay byte-identical (RC-M1/M2, DEC-234).
**Files.** `prompts.py`, `schemas.py`, `steps/script.py`, `steps/judge.py`, `timing.py` (`word_budget_v3` only), `templates/episodes/confrontation_50s_v2.json`, `defaults.py` (`EPISODE_TEMPLATE_IDS`), `web/dashboard/src/pages/story/episodeTemplates.js`, `web/dashboard/src/pages/story/episode/ScriptPane.jsx`.
**Design.**
- Template: `window_s [44, 58]`, `target_s 50`, `tighten_above_s 55`, `scenes [4, 6]`, `shots [9, 16]`, `min_shot_s 2`, `max_shot_s 8`; slots hook 4–8 s, body count [2, 3] of 10–16 s, cliffhanger 6–10 s, recap 3–4 s from episode 2. New optional template keys (schema + cross-checks): `single_place: true` (E1 told one continuous place, `max_places` 1), `scene_transition: "cut"` (`timing.plan_transitions` cuts only when the key is present), `narrator_slots: ["recap"]` (amends DEC-231 for this format only), `line_words: [5, 17]`, `episode_words: [95, 125]`, `reaction_shots: [0, 1]`.
- E1v3 = E1v2 + a spine first: `spine.logline` (one complete sentence ≤ 30 words: who wants what, what they do, where it leaves them), `want`, `obstacle`, `stakes` (≤ 15 words each), `turn` (≤ 20); summaries "one or two complete sentences, at most 30 words, saying what happens and why — what it follows from, what is done, what it changes for the next scene. Never a mood, a title or a list. Read in order, the summaries retell spine.logline." On `single_place`: "the episode is one continuous scene in one place, in real time; the scenes are its beats, cut together with no time jump. The antagonist drives it with demands and accusations, each with its reason; the target answers little; it ends right before the threatened act happens." `MAX_TOKENS["E1v3"] = 2100`; `validate_e1` caps; optional `spine` on the script document.
- E2v3 ask: the episode's every line so far (last 220 words, cut named), the spine, this scene's and the next scene's summary; "{n_lo} to {n_hi} lines … each text is one or two complete sentences in {language}, {w_lo} to {w_hi} words, that this person would say aloud right now. Each line does one job the story needs — a demand, an accusation, a fact the viewer did not know, a refusal, a threat or a reveal — and moves the scene toward the next. Say the reason behind every demand or accusation in the line itself ('because…', 'since you…', 'the more you…, the more…'). No filler (no lone 'Quoi ?', 'Écoute', a name alone), no line that restates an earlier one, no stage directions in the text." On a native-speech story: "Each line is spoken on camera by its speaker in one shot of at most 8 seconds: at most 17 words." `INPUT_BUDGET["E2v3"] ≈ 2600`, `MAX_TOKENS` 700. `timing.word_budget_v3` spreads `episode_words` by duration share, lines by `line_words`; without `episode_words`: `target_s × 2.4 × (1 − silent share)`. The narrated lines lose "short … each a punch" in v3.
- E3v3 = E3v2 + the line rule, spine, dialogue so far; on the confrontation format the cliffhanger's last line states the threatened act.
- J1v3: the spine in the digest; new kinds `line_no_progress`, `incomplete_sentence`, `logline_mismatch` (list widened, stored reports valid); pre-checks: a character line under 3 words → minor `incomplete_sentence`; a line outside `line_words` → blocking; `J1_PROMPT_VERSION` 3.
- `ScriptPane.jsx`: "What happens" (the spine's logline) above the scenes, want/stakes/turn under it.
**Risk.** Longer prompts on the free tail (budgets measured); `scene_transition` touches `plan_transitions` (gated by key presence, goldens prove it).
**Verification.** New `tests/test_story_prompts_v3.py`, `tests/test_story_confrontation_template.py`; `test_story_judge.py` (new kinds, pre-checks); `test_aistory_templates.py` ids 5 → 6 re-pinned on purpose. Unedited: `test_story_prompts_episode.py`, `test_aistory_render_golden*.py`; at risk: `test_story_episode_prompt_budgets.py`, `test_story_first_watch_*`, `test_story_timing.py`.
**Rollback.** Revert; `writing: v3` stories fall back to v2 (optional key). **RC:** RC-M1, RC-M2, RC-M3, new RC-W3 "a story without `writing: v3` writes, judges and times byte-identically".

### Stage 4 — the native-speech pipeline core (riskiest stage; Opus agent; link-agnostic)
**Goal.** On a speech story each character line is one shot whose clip speaks it on the speech link; silent shots on the silent link of the same family; shots planned at the lengths the link sells; no TTS and no lipsync for on-screen lines; the narrator stays TTS voice-over; subtitles timed by aligning the clip's own audio; one take per shot, flagged shots retaken once. Everything here works the same whether the clip comes from an API link (stage 6) or from the human (stage 5): the shot plan, the speech prompt, the native take, the timing pass and the render read the clip and its `assets.clip` record, never the link. Until stage 6 lands, the `speech_links` / `silent_link` of the profile resolve to `manual/upload` (stage 5); the Lite link already exists for ambience.
**Choice.** A new budget profile `native_speech` (not a mode inside `quality`): it changes the cap, lipsync, the link policy, the TTS requirement and the timing source at once, and every existing quality story stays byte-identical.
```json
"native_speech": {"label": "Native speech (Veo, billed)", "cap_usd": 10.0, "images": "quality_roles",
  "roles": <quality's>, "tts": "free_or_local", "animate": "all_shots",
  "video_link_policy": "speech_by_shot",
  "speech_links": {"lite": "gemini/veo-3.1-lite", "fast": "gemini/veo-3.1-fast", "premium": "gemini/veo-3.1"},
  "speech_model": "fast", "silent_link": "gemini/veo-3.1-lite", "video_resolution": "720p",
  "tier3_native_audio": "speech", "keyframe_fix": {"max_redraws_per_shot": 2, "cap_usd": 0.40},
  "speech_retake": {"max_per_shot": 1, "cap_usd": 1.0}, "lipsync": "none"}
```
`generation_profile.speech_model` ∈ lite | fast | premium (per-story switch, like `lipsync`); `media_policy.native_speech(story)` = v2 + tier 3 + `tier3_native_audio == "speech"`.
**Files.** `templates/budget_profiles.json`, `providers/budget.py`, `aistory/defaults.py`, `schemas.py`, `media_policy.py`, `steps/storyboard.py` + `shots.py`, `steps/clips.py`, `prompting.py` + `prompt_budgets.py`, `steps/sticky_link.py`, `steps/assets.py`, `timing.py` (`native_pass`), `render/timeline.py`, `steps/render.py`, `render/plan.py` / `filtergraph.py`, `voice_lines.py`, `workflow.py`, dashboard `GenerationProfileCard.jsx`, `NewStoryWizard.jsx`, the Video card.
**Design.**
1. Shot plan (`shots.speech_shot_plan`, only on `native_speech`; T1v2 still writes framing/action): each character line → one shot `speaks: true` (speaker subject, listener secondary), `clip_s` = smallest sold length with `capacity(L) = floor((L − 0.7) × SPEECH_WPS)` (2.4 until the probe: 4 s → 7 words, 6 → 12, 8 → 17); ≤ `reaction_shots` per scene `speaks: false` 4 s; a narrator line → a silent shot with TTS voice-over sized to the measured narration; every shot's `duration_s == clip_s` (nothing bought is cut; DEC-250 stretch and DEC-208 hold never on a speech shot); storyboard stamps `timing_mode: "native_speech"`.
2. Links per class: speaking → `speech_links[speech_model]`, silent → `silent_link`; `assets.links.video` + new optional `assets.links.video_speech`, each sticky (RC-V5 amended: two links per episode by class, both in the estimate). Seedance as `silent_link` is a per-story override (saves $0.34, different look).
3. Speech clip prompt (`prompting.speech_clip_prompt`, ceiling 200 words): "{Speaker}, {look ≤ 12 words}, {action}, looks at {listener} and says in {language}, in {voice line}: "{line}". {Listener} listens without speaking, mouth closed, {reaction}. {camera}. {IDENTITY_KEEPS} {style motion suffix} Audio: only {speaker}'s voice speaking {language}, close and clear, lips in sync with the words; the {place} ambience low underneath; no music, no narrator, no other voice. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text." `voice_line(character)` built once from `voice_hints` and identical in every clip of that character (voice-drift mitigation); the quoted line, voice line, Audio and no-subtitles sentences are never dropped. Silent shots use the ambience prompt.
4. No TTS or lipsync for on-screen lines; the assets step voices narrator lines only.
5. Native take (`assets._Assets.native_take_shot`, free, after `apply_clip`): extract the clip's audio, transcribe with `default_transcriber` (story language), `wordtiming.align`; check matched ≥ 0.75 and last word ≤ `clip_s − 0.1`; write the line's audio as the extracted segment + sidecar `words_source: alignment` so `voice_lines.is_measured` and the render precondition hold unchanged; record `assets.clip.native_speech = {state ok|mismatch|no_speech|stt_unavailable, matched, heard, start_s, end_s}`; mismatch/no_speech → flagged like a J2 issue, one paid retake (agent mode within `speech_retake.cap_usd`); `stt_unavailable` → even-split over the planned window, labelled approximate, the feed names the missing key.
6. Timing and render: `timing.native_pass` only when `timing_mode == "native_speech"` (fixed `clip_s` durations, a native line at shot start + take `start_s`, narrator lines as today); `render/plan.py` puts a speech shot in DEC-201's `video_native_audio` mode (`clip_in_s = 0`), a silent shot in `video_ambience` with the narrator ducking it; subtitles read the aligned sidecars unchanged; existing boards never take this path.
7. Estimate/caps: `clips.video_units` prices speaking seconds × speech price + silent seconds × silent price + retake contingency + `text_usd`; `preset_estimate` gains the branch (so `_agent_episode_usd` stops falling back to `cap_usd`); the fast track refuses before spending unless every cap fits; shipped defaults unchanged (DEC-223); `premium` (≈ $17) refused by a $10 cap with the number.
8. Dashboard: the wizard offers "Native speech (Veo)" with the speech-model select and estimate, naming the missing keys; the Video card names both links and prices, each shot's `speaks` badge and take state ("🗣 matched 92 %").
**Risk.** Changes the render's timing source and line precondition for one profile; amends RC-V5; depends on STT alignment of Veo's French; must leave every golden byte-identical. Mitigations: stage 4's probe first, the `timing_mode`/`native_speech` gates, real-ffmpeg render tests on fixture clips, the speech check before render.

**Design.**
- **The brief** (`assets/brief/shot_brief.json` + `.md`, one entry per shot, and one per image when images are manual): the final clip prompt as stage 4 builds it (speech or ambience), rephrased by the platform preset (Flow: "Use quotes for speech", captions off; Higgsfield: the model's own reference syntax), the negative prompt, the length to pick (Flow 8 s; Higgsfield per model) and the aspect, the reference images in priority order (the shot's keyframe when one exists, else the speaker's sheet, the listener's sheet, the place plate; cut to the platform's maximum), the line(s) in the story language with the voice line, the shot's purpose in one sentence (so the human can judge a take: "Rouge's first accusation; she leans in; Nude freezes"), and what to check before uploading (lips on the words, no captions, the look). The `.md` is written for a human: numbered shots, copy-ready prompt blocks, image file names. The zip carries the `.md` and the reference images.
- **The dashboard "Shot list" pane**: per shot the prompt with a copy button, the reference thumbnails with download, the line, an upload slot, the state (missing / uploaded / take ok / take mismatch); a progress line "7 of 12 clips uploaded"; "Download brief (zip)"; the Generate button reads "Waiting for 5 clips" until every speaking and silent shot has one. Images (sheets, plates, props, keyframes) get the same treatment on their tiles when the image link is manual.
- **Upload validation** (ffprobe): a video track, ≥ 2 s, 9:16 (or croppable to it: the render's existing scale/crop), a sound track for a speaking shot; refused with the reason otherwise. Stored as `assets/clips/shot_NN.manual.mp4`; re-upload replaces (the old take kept under `takes/`), the lipsync/native-take records go stale by sha256 as today.
- **Length rule**: the brief's length is a target, the uploaded clip's real length is the shot's duration; a speaking shot is trimmed after the last aligned word + 0.3 s only when the clip runs more than 1 s past it (so an 8 s Flow clip of a 4-word line does not drag); a silent shot keeps its length. RC-N3 reads "every uploaded second is rendered or trimmed by that rule".
- **Resume**: the assets step lists the missing uploads and ends `awaiting_uploads`; an upload re-runs the step for that shot (native take) and, when none is missing, the fast track continues to the render. The ledger books nothing for a manual link.
**Risk.** A pausing job in agent mode (reuse the approval-gate pattern); platform drift in prompt syntax (presets are data files); the human's clips off-spec (validation refuses with the reason).
