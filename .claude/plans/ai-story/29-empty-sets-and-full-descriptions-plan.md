## 1. The trigger and the root causes

Story 51dbc4213738, Places step, 2026-10-06 06:20–06:31 UTC (activity.log 309–472):
- "Bureau ultramoderne de Clara's day plate does not match: The image contains fruit characters, but it must be an empty set without any persons. Regenerate it, or upload your own." after two automatic redraws ($0.04 each, fal/seedream-4.5). The second place failed the same way and passed on redraw 2; the prop "Terminal de piratage portable" failed with an apple character in it.
- The human's Regenerate at 06:30:49 drew a new plate (seed 1429133984), the judge passed it at 06:31:06 — the tile kept showing the old picture.

1. **The plate prompt invites the characters.** `refimages._sent` → `prompt_templates.entity_prompt` prepends ART STYLE whose first sentence is `_FRUIT_PEOPLE` ("The characters are fruit and vegetable people: each one's whole head IS the fruit…"); the style lock's `rendering` ("photorealistic 3D render of anthropomorphic fruits and vegetables with expressive human-like faces…") sits inside the core; the SERIES section (when kept) adds "Universe: every character is an anthropomorphic fruit". Against ≈ 440 words of fruit people the only exclusion is `plate_prompt_v2`'s head "Establishing wide shot of an empty set, day, no people, no characters:" plus the closing "no people" — two negations, which image models read as subjects. The Scale line ("comfortably fitting several people") does not help. Props: same medium sentence, "the object alone" only in the core.
2. **No negative prompt reaches the image links.** `GenRequest.negative` is filled (`refimages.py:703`, `prompting.negative_prompt`) but fal seedream-4.5 / -edit have no such field (A-111, `providers/images.py:276`), Gemini's image API has none, Pollinations ignores it; only local ComfyUI and some video links read it. The exclusion must be positive prose, first in the core.
3. **The automatic redraw feeds the fault back as words.** `sheet_gate._redraw` uses a fresh seed and appends `"Fix what the last picture got wrong: " + issues` — i.e. "…contains fruit characters…" — to a prompt that already invites them. `REDRAWS = 2`; the ceiling prices a plate redraw as `role="sheet"` (portrait size), not as a plate.
4. **The tile never reloads a regenerated picture.** `variant_day.jpg` keeps its name; `VariantSlot`'s `useEffect` depends on `imageRef.name` only (`PlacesStep.jsx ~274–295`; same pattern `CastStep.jsx:217/294`, props `PlacesStep.jsx:607`). The server sends `no-store` and a new file; the blob URL in state is the old one.
5. **No element carries a written description.** Characters are a stack of 8–20-word fields (`look`: build 15, face 15, hair 12…; master paragraph 143–208 words), places descriptor 45 + layout_notes 60 + look fields of 15 (≈ 105 in the master), props descriptor 30 + material 8 + colour 6 + scale 10 (≈ 40). Every cap is written three times (the `*_ASK` text, the `_llm_obj` schema descriptions, the `*_MAX_WORDS` constants) and `MAX_TOKENS` is pinned by tests.
6. **No approve-anyway on cast / places / props** (`stories.py:1976`: approve_anyway applies to script and keyframes only; `workflow.approve_entity` raises CONFLICT on a `failed` or `unjudged` slot — DEC-305 point 5), while DEC-307 (2026-10-06) says the human approves what they want and is only warned.
