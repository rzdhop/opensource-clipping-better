# Suzon

## Head
Suzon, a plasticine hedgehog woman in her sixties: a round warm-brown clay head with a crown of short stubby clay spines, small black bead eyes behind round gold-rimmed glasses, rosy cheeks and a gentle closed smile; a plump little body in a pale pink striped baker's smock over a lavender blouse, a white cotton headscarf, a sprig of dried lavender pinned to her chest, four-fingered clay hands, flat brown shoes

## Voice (en)
an older woman, soft and warm, slow and careful, a voice that has learned to say little

## Voice (fr)
une femme âgée, voix douce et chaleureuse, lente et prudente, qui a appris à peu parler

## Colour
#F7A1C4

## Wants
That the Fournil stays open and nobody in the family gets hurt by what she knows.

## Fears
The day Mireille learns why Gaspard trusted her.

## Secret
Gaspard left her the bakery because Mireille is not his daughter: Suzon knows who the father is.

## How they speak
Soft, few words, always 'mademoiselle' and 'monsieur' to the children she raised. Answers a question with a kindness.

## Signature item
The sprig of dried lavender on her smock (Gaspard's, every Sunday).
