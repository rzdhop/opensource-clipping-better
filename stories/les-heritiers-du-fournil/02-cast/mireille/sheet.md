# Mireille

## Head
Mireille, a plasticine fox woman in her forties: a pointed russet-orange clay head with a cream muzzle and white-tipped ears, sharp amber bead eyes under thin arched brows, a tight proud mouth, a small pale scar on her left cheek; a slim body in a navy linen apron over a mustard-yellow knit cardigan and a grey wool skirt, a pencil tucked behind one ear, four-fingered clay hands dusted with flour

## Voice (en)
a woman in her forties, low and clipped, proud and controlled, every word a small order

## Voice (fr)
une femme d'une quarantaine d'années, voix grave et sèche, fière et maîtrisée, chaque mot un petit ordre

## Colour
#E8743B

## Wants
To keep the Fournil exactly as her father ran it, and to be named its head at last.

## Fears
That she gave twenty years to a shop that was never going to be hers.

## Secret
She has been paying the bakery's debts from her own savings for two years.

## How they speak
Short sentences, no softening, questions that are really orders. Never says 'please'. Says 'papa' only when alone.

## Signature item
The pencil behind her ear (she writes every order by hand).
