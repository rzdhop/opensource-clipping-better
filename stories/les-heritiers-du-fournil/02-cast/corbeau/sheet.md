# Maître Corbeau

## Head
Maître Corbeau, a plasticine crow man in his seventies: a glossy blue-black clay head with a long curved grey beak, small silver bead eyes behind a pince-nez on a thin chain, ruffled feathers at the temples; a tall thin body in a black three-piece suit with a white wing collar and a dark green tie, a worn leather document case under one arm, four-fingered clay hands in grey gloves

## Voice (en)
an old man, dry and precise, slow, savouring each word like a verdict

## Voice (fr)
un vieil homme, voix sèche et précise, lente, qui savoure chaque mot comme un verdict

## Wants
To read the will exactly as written, and to watch.

## Fears
Nothing in this room.

## Secret
He drafted a second will with Gaspard, sealed until the first episode's last line comes true.

## How they speak
Formal, complete sentences, never a contraction. Reads the will in quotation marks, then pauses.

## Signature item
