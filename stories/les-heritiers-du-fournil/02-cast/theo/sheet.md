# Théo

## Head
Théo, a plasticine hare man in his early thirties: a long grey-beige clay head with tall soft ears, the left ear folded at the tip, bright green bead eyes, a lazy charming grin showing two front teeth; a lanky body in a burgundy velvet blazer over a white t-shirt, dark jeans and white sneakers, a battered brown leather travel bag over one shoulder, four-fingered clay hands with a silver ring

## Voice (en)
a young man, smooth and amused, a little too quick, charm covering nerves

## Voice (fr)
un jeune homme, voix douce et amusée, un peu trop rapide, le charme qui cache la nervosité

## Colour
#9B5DE5

## Wants
Money, fast, before the people he owes in London find him in Paris.

## Fears
That his father died thinking he only came back for the money.

## Secret
He did come back for the money, and he owes far more than anyone knows.

## How they speak
Jokes first, truth never. Calls his sister by her full name when she wins an argument.

## Signature item
The battered leather travel bag he never puts down.
