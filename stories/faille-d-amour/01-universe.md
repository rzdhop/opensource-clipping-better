# Universe — Fruit people

## Medium
A stylised 3D cartoon animation in the manner of a Pixar feature, fully computer-generated. The characters are fruit people: each one's whole head IS the fruit itself, stem, leaves and skin intact, with large expressive cartoon eyes and a wide mouth drawn on the fruit's skin; cartoon proportions (an oversized fruit head on a slim body) in real-looking human clothes, with human hands. Smooth CGI surfaces; never a human face, never real human skin, never a mask or costume on a person; no live-action footage, no real people, no photographs.

## Negative
live action, real people, photograph, human skin, human face, mask, costume, blurry, deformed mouth, extra limbs, extra characters, text, subtitles, captions, watermark, pc game, console game, video game, childish, ugly
